Draft — for review only. This Agreement sets the terms on which the Association licenses the SHANHAI TONGWEN (山海同文) master brand to the Licensee within the cooperation — non-exclusive, non-transferable, quality-controlled and revocable; ownership of the brand always remains with the Association. Usage rules are governed by the SHANHAI TONGWEN Brand Book. It is not legal advice and must be finalised by a Singapore-qualified lawyer and after trademark clearance/registration. Parties & recitals

Licensor (brand owner): Global Association of Cultural and Educational Exchange ("GACEE" / the "Association"), owner of the SHANHAI TONGWEN master brand, [reg. no./address]

Licensee: [JE AI / local entity / school using the brand]

Whereas the Association owns the SHANHAI TONGWEN brand and the Licensee needs to use it in the cooperation, the Parties enter into this Agreement.

1. Brand assets & ownership

  1. Brand assets: the Chinese wordmark 「山海同文」, the English wordmark "SHANHAI TONGWEN", the seal device, the standard colours and visual system, etc. (see the Brand Book).
  2. Ownership: the brand assets and their goodwill always belong to the Association; this Agreement grants a licence only, and is no assignment or co-ownership.
  3. Bare-use restriction: the English form must not be used as a bare "SHANHAI" (to avoid confusion with Shanghai); the full "SHANHAI TONGWEN" must be used.

2. Grant of licence

  1. The Association grants the Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, revocable licence, limited to [scope / project / medium] and [territory].
  2. Permitted uses: project promotion, platform and course naming, certificates and materials, and other scenarios approved by the Association.
  3. Licence fee: [none / nominal / as agreed] (as a non-profit, the Association typically grants brand endorsement in exchange for quality compliance rather than a fee).

3. Quality control & standards

  1. The Licensee's use must comply with the Brand Book and the Association's educational standards; major uses (new media, new market, co-branding) require the Association's prior written approval.
  2. The Association may inspect brand use; non-compliant use must be remedied within [ days ].
  3. Co-branding must carry the endorsement form "SHANHAI TONGWEN — An initiative of GACEE" (see the Brand Book).

4. Usage rules

  1. Do not modify, distort or split the marks or change the standard colours / proportions (proportional scaling where legally/technically necessary excepted).
  2. Do not use the brand for products/services outside the cooperation, or for political / religious / discriminatory content.
  3. Use of the Association's seal requires separate authorisation and must not be used unilaterally.

5. Goodwill & prohibitions

  1. All goodwill arising from brand use accrues to the Association.
  2. The Licensee shall not: register any trademark / domain / trade name identical or similar to SHANHAI TONGWEN; challenge the Association's brand rights; or harm the brand's reputation.

6. Infringement & enforcement

  1. On becoming aware of third-party infringement, the Licensee promptly notifies the Association; whether and how to enforce is decided and led by the Association, with the Licensee's cooperation.
  2. Trademark clearance / registration (IPOS and each pilot country) is pursued by the Association; costs and arrangements agreed separately.

7. Term, revocation & termination

  1. The licence term matches the Framework / relevant PSA, or [as separately agreed].
  2. If the Licensee misuses the brand, harms its reputation or breaches this Agreement and fails to cure after notice, the Association may immediately suspend or revoke the licence.
  3. On termination, the Licensee must immediately cease use and remove the marks and materials (a reasonable transition period to be agreed).

8. Governing law & disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore; disputes go to SIAC in Singapore.
