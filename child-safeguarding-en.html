Child Safeguarding Policy
Applies to all personnel

Schedule to the Framework · Governing law: Singapore + local law · v1.0 (draft, subject to legal review)

Draft — for review only. This Policy applies to all personnel who come into contact with minor learners in SHANHAI TONGWEN AI Chinese learning platform projects (teachers, teaching assistants, operations, volunteers, contractors, sub-processor staff). Signing the Framework / PSA constitutes acceptance. It is not legal advice and must be localised against each pilot country's child-protection law. Square brackets [ ] require completion.

1. Purpose & scope

  1. Purpose: to keep every minor learner safe and treated with dignity in the project (online and offline), and to prevent any harm, abuse, neglect, exploitation or harassment.
  2. Scope: all personnel and situations involving minors or their data — remote live class, recordings, offline class, messaging, audio/video and homework marking.
  3. Zero tolerance: zero tolerance for any abuse of or misconduct towards children; on confirmation, the person is removed from duties immediately and dealt with under law.

2. Principles

Child's best interests

The child's best interests are the primary consideration in every decision.

Equality & respect

No discrimination by gender, ethnicity, religion or ability; respect culture and mother tongue.

Safe environment

A safe, supervisable and traceable environment online and offline.

Informed consent

Processing children's data requires verifiable guardian consent (see the DPA).

3. Recruitment & background checks

  1. Safe recruitment for roles in contact with children: verify identity and qualifications, take references, and state safeguarding responsibilities.
  2. Criminal / background checks per local law ([e.g., the host country's criminal-record check]); records kept and periodically reviewed.
  3. Personnel may start only after signing this Policy and the code of conduct; enhanced observation during probation.

4. Code of conduct (Do / Don't)

Do

  • Communicate with learners and guardians via official, logged channels.
  • Keep classes and tutoring visible/observable; 1:1 sessions must be recorded or known to a third person.
  • Report concerns immediately to the Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL).
  • Obtain guardian consent before using audio/video or learner work, for teaching only.

Don't

  • No private, unlogged one-to-one contact or personal social-media links with learners.
  • Do not solicit or share learners' private images / personal information for non-teaching purposes.
  • No inappropriate physical, verbal or online contact with learners.
  • Do not conceal, delay or privately handle any safeguarding incident.

5. Online safety

  1. In-platform communication runs through supervised channels; exchanging private contact details between teacher and learner is discouraged.
  2. Live/recorded and homework audio/video are handled under data minimisation and the DPA; teacher-voice cloning is limited to the class and deletable.
  3. Mechanisms to detect and handle suspected cyberbullying, grooming or boundary-crossing content; AI-generated content is safety-filtered.

6. Reporting & response

Anyone who sees or suspects harm to a child must report immediately; do not investigate alone or conceal.
  1. Channel: report to the Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) [name / contact]; in emergencies contact local law-enforcement / child-protection authorities directly.
  2. Response: DSL assesses → protective measures → notify authorities per local law → cooperate with investigation → document throughout.
  3. Whistle-blower protection: good-faith reporters are protected from retaliation.

7. Roles & training

All relevant personnel receive safeguarding training before starting, then refresher training at least annually.

8. Records & review

  1. Background checks, training, reports and responses are recorded and auditable; incident records kept per local law.
  2. This Policy is reviewed annually, or updated immediately on a serious incident or legal change.
