Data Processing Addendum
Including minors' data

Schedule to the Framework / PSA · Governing law: Singapore PDPA + local law · v1.0 (draft)

Draft — for review only. This Addendum sets the rules for processing personal data (including minors' learner data) on the platform, forms part of the Framework / PSA and prevails on data terms. Square brackets [ ] require completion.

Parties & roles

Controller: [the party determining purposes & means — typically the project school / the Association]

Processor: Juris&Edu AI Technology Pte. Ltd. ("JE AI"), the platform operator processing data on the Controller's instructions.

Roles are determined per project on the facts; if the Association only provides pedagogy and endorsement and does not determine purposes, the school is Controller and the Association is a separate controller or not involved.

1. Definitions

  1. Personal data: any information identifying a natural person — learner names, contacts, homework and answers, voice and images, learning behaviour and mastery data, etc.
  2. Minor: a learner below the age of majority under local law ([ages per each country's law]).
  3. Processing: any operation on personal data — collection, recording, storage, use, transfer, deletion, etc.
  4. Sub-processor: a third party engaged by the Processor to process personal data (e.g., model gateway, voice synthesis, cloud and CDN providers).

2. Scope & instructions

  1. Purpose limitation: data is processed only for teaching delivery and learning analytics; not for out-of-project purposes or marketing without consent.
  2. On instructions: the Processor processes only on the Controller's documented instructions; this Addendum and the PSA are the initial instructions.
  3. Data minimisation: collect only what is necessary; de-identify where possible (platform PII de-identification).
  4. Personnel confidentiality: those with access are bound by confidentiality and trained.

3. Minors' data (priority)

Learners are mostly minors; this clause is a hard requirement.
  1. Guardian consent: obtain verifiable parent/guardian consent before processing minors' data; consent is logged and withdrawable.
  2. Minimisation & purpose: teaching only; no profiling-based marketing, no sale to third parties.
  3. Voice / video: teacher-voice cloning and classroom media require separate authorisation, are limited to that class, and are deletable.
  4. Deletion / export: guardians may request export or deletion of their child's data (subject to legal retention).

4. Security measures

  1. Encryption in transit and at rest; least-privilege access control and audit trails; periodic security assessment.
  2. Data residency: default [Singapore]; PII de-identified before entering the model pipeline.
  3. Backup and recovery, key management, endpoint and account security; local data under weak-network/offline scenarios is equally protected.

5. Sub-processors

  1. The Processor may engage sub-processors, provided it: gives the Controller prior written notice and obtains general or specific consent; and imposes data-protection obligations no less protective than this Addendum (flow-down).
  2. Current sub-processor list (illustrative): [model gateway / voice synthesis / cloud & CDN providers]; changes on prior notice, with the Controller able to object on reasonable grounds.
  3. The Processor remains responsible to the Controller for its sub-processors.

6. Cross-border transfers

  1. Any transfer out of the jurisdiction requires a lawful basis (adequacy, standard contractual clauses / binding rules, or consent, etc.) and appropriate safeguards.
  2. Comply with local-law transfer requirements ([Vietnam PDPD / Indonesia UU PDP / Malaysia PDPA / Singapore PDPA]); transfer records are kept.

7. Data-subject rights

  1. The Processor assists the Controller with data-subject (or guardian) requests — access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent — responding within [a reasonable period].
  2. It shall not impede the exercise of statutory rights.

8. Breach notification

  1. On an actual or suspected personal-data breach, the Processor notifies the Controller no later than [72] hours after becoming aware, describing nature, scope, impact and measures taken/proposed.
  2. It cooperates with notifications to regulators and affected individuals as required by local law.

9. Audit & records

  1. The Processor keeps records of processing and, on reasonable notice, supports the Controller's audit/verification (third-party audit reports may substitute for on-site audits).
  2. Retention period and format per local law and the Compliance · Legal · Finance advisory §04.

10. Term & post-termination

  1. This Addendum lasts as long as the Framework / PSA.
  2. On termination, the Processor returns or deletes all personal data and copies at the Controller's choice (subject to legal retention) and certifies it.

11. Governing law & disputes

This Addendum is governed by the laws of Singapore together with each pilot country's data law; disputes go to SIAC in Singapore. Mandatory local-law provisions prevail.
