Schedule 1 to the Tripartite Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement · Governing law: Singapore + Nigerian local law · v0.1 (draft skeleton)

Draft skeleton — for internal and negotiation use only. The project-specific agreement ("PSA") under the Tripartite Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement (the "Framework") for the Africa (Nigeria) Chinese-learning project (the "Project"). The Framework sets the principles; this PSA sets the commercial terms, responsibilities, technical specifications, service levels and acceptance for the Project. Key positions are built in: the Platform is Party A's Background IP provided under a non-transferable licence (not bespoke Foreground IP), and there is no core source-code escrow (business continuity is delivered through data/content portability) — see Clauses 5 and 10. Whereas: (A) the Parties signed the Framework on [date]; (B) the Parties wish, under Clause 4 of the Framework, to run and progressively expand a Chinese-learning project in Nigeria and other parts of Africa (the "Project"); (C) Party A owns the intellectual property in its self-developed AI Chinese learning platform and will provide that platform and related services to the Project on a licensed basis. The Parties agree as follows.

1. Definitions & interpretation

Platform
Party A's self-developed and owned AI Chinese learning platform, including its JE Engine, JE mixed-orchestration agent array, TIDAR-KAG low-hallucination retrieval, model orchestration, algorithms, source code, architecture and data models, and Party A's updates and improvements to it.
Background IP
IP existing before, or independently developed by Party A outside, this PSA, including the Platform.
Foreground IP
Deliverables developed specifically for the Project at Party B/C's written request that are not part of the Platform's general capabilities (to be construed narrowly — see Clause 5).
Project Content
Chinese courses, pedagogy, teaching methods, HSK learning system, question banks and brand assets supplied or created by the Association or its authorised parties.
Project Data
Learner profiles, attendance, assignments, assessments, learning behaviour, progress, ILAPs and reports generated in the Project.
Specifications
The functional and technical requirements in Schedule 1 (Technical Specification Annex).

Interpretation: headings are for convenience; "including" means "including without limitation"; the singular includes the plural; a reference to a statute is to that statute as amended; conflicts are resolved under Clause 2.

2. Documents & order of precedence

This PSA is Schedule 1 to the Framework and, together with it, forms the entire agreement for the Project. In case of conflict, the order of precedence is:

  1. for Project commercial terms (fees, funding, splits, scale): this PSA prevails over the Framework;
  2. for IP, brand, data protection, compliance and dispute resolution: the Framework prevails;
  3. this PSA prevails over its Schedules; as between Schedules, in their listed order.

3. Scope & phasing

  1. Scope. Chinese-learning services to [grade / HSK entry level] learners at [Nigeria / city / school] on a "live teacher + digital learning + AI tutoring + continuous assessment + personalised intervention" model.
  2. Phased delivery. The Project is delivered in phases. The Phase 1 (MVP) scope is as set out in Schedule 1 and Schedule 4 (Acceptance & Milestones); it is not a one-time delivery of every Schedule 1 feature at production depth. Later capabilities follow via change control (Clause 17).
  3. Educational target. The design target is to help learners reach HSK 4 in about [2] years; this is not a guarantee of any learner's exam results or official HSK certification (Clause 13).
  4. Out of scope. Official HSK registration/certification, study-abroad admissions custody, and anything not listed in this PSA.

5. Platform licence & IP

Core clause. The Platform is Party A's Background IP, provided under licence; it is not "Foreground/bespoke IP" and no assignment or co-ownership passes to Party B/C.
  1. Background IP. The Platform and all of its components (engine, agent orchestration, TIDAR-KAG, algorithms, source code, architecture, data models, AI models) are Party A's Background IP and remain owned by Party A. This PSA effects no assignment or co-ownership. "Bespoke/Foreground" characterisation does not apply to the Platform's general capabilities.
  2. Licence grant. Party A grants Party B, Party C and the Local Counterparty a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, revocable licence, limited to the Project scope and licensed territory, to use and promote the Platform. No Party shall copy, modify, reverse-engineer, sell, sub-licence or otherwise dispose of the Platform or Party A's IP without Party A's written consent.
  3. Project Content / Project Data. Project Content belongs to the Association or its authorised parties; Project Data belongs to the Association or the client (the data-rights holder) and is processed per Schedule 5 (DPA). Party A is a processor and acquires no ownership by providing the services.
  4. Foreground IP. Any deliverable developed specifically for the Project beyond the Platform's general capabilities is allocated item-by-item in Schedule 2; by default, anything that is an extension of the Platform's underlying capabilities belongs to Party A and is folded into the Platform.
  5. Feedback licence. Party A may freely and perpetually use feedback and suggestions to improve the Platform.
  6. Brand. The "SHANHAI TONGWEN" brand belongs to the Association, used under the Brand Licence, quality-controlled and revocable; the English form must be the full name, never a bare "SHANHAI".

6. Specifications & acceptance

  1. Phase 1 of the Platform shall materially conform to the MVP requirements in Schedule 1. Items marked "in principle / where technically feasible / subject to PoC" are design goals, not hard guarantees.
  2. Acceptance. The Parties shall jointly and in writing set the acceptance criteria and UAT cases at Project initiation, aligned to the MVP scope and to Nigerian low-bandwidth/power-outage/low-spec realities (Schedule 4).
  3. Party A completes testing per Schedule 4 (functional, integration, security, performance, low-bandwidth, offline, network/power-recovery, low-spec Android, AI, UAT) before hand-over; defects are remedied by severity.

7. Service levels (SLA)

  1. Target monthly production availability [99.5%] (excluding planned maintenance). Response/repair times and RTO/RPO are in Schedule 3.
  2. Boundary of responsibility. The SLA covers Platform faults only; local internet outages, power outages, third-party meeting-platform/network/CDN failures and force majeure do not count as SLA breaches.
  3. SLA tiers are matched to the commercial terms (Schedule 2).

8. Commercial terms, funding & settlement

Key open item. The specification is silent on commercials; the Project's development funding, licence/service fees and revenue split are decisive and must be set in Schedule 2 — not invented.
  1. Funding. Development funding for Platform adaptation and delivery is [borne by Party B / the funder / split by ratio], per Schedule 2.
  2. Fees. Licence/service billing model [per seat·month / per class / per-school package / subscription]; unit price and currency per Schedule 2.
  3. Collection & no commingling. Learner/Project fees are collected by [JE AI / a local commercial entity]; no individual may collect on the side; Association funds go to an account in the Association's name with zero commingling, and the Association distributes no surplus from commercial services.
  4. Settlement. Reconciled every [month / quarter] with vouchers and an AI usage/cost report (Schedule 3); disputed amounts held over, the rest paid on time.

9. Data & child protection

  1. The Project involves minors' data. Obtain verifiable parent/guardian consent before processing; process under Schedule 5 (DPA) and local law (Nigeria NDPA 2023 / NDPR) and Singapore PDPA.
  2. The Parties observe the Child Safeguarding Policy (Schedule 6); personnel in contact with learners are background-checked and trained; AI outputs carry minor-safety guardrails.
  3. Data minimisation; cross-border transfers require a lawful basis and safeguards; breaches notified within the DPA time limits.

10. Business continuity & data portability

Core clause (replaces source-code escrow). What the client needs is continuity of service and control of its data/content — not possession of Party A's source code.
  1. No core source-code escrow. Party A is not required to place the Platform's core source code in third-party escrow.
  2. Data & content portability. On request and on termination, Party A shall provide the data-rights holder with an export of Project Data, Project Content and system configuration in a common, structured format, with reasonable migration assistance, and shall not technically impede this.
  3. Continuity. Party A maintains backups and disaster recovery (Schedule 3). If Party A ceases business, cannot continue the service, or is in long-term material breach uncured after notice, Party A shall grant a reasonable transition period and assist migration of data/content/configuration so the Project can continue.
  4. Optional enhancement (if the client insists). Escrow or a "continuity operator" arrangement may be set up over Project Data + deployment configuration + documentation (excluding the Platform's core source code), on terms to be agreed.

11. AI governance & model use

  1. Human oversight. AI analyses, suggests and assists; authorised teachers/administrators retain final teaching judgment; important AI determinations are not permanently locked without a human-review mechanism; teachers may modify/override/correct/report AI outputs.
  2. Training restriction. Party A shall not, without the data-rights holder's written authorisation, use learner/child data, conversation logs or Project Content to train third-party general AI models; de-identified processing within the service to provide and improve it is not so restricted (per the DPA).
  3. Model disclosure. Party A reasonably discloses the third-party models used, data-processing locations and whether data is used for training; but does not disclose its proprietary orchestration, prompt engineering or weights. Material changes to a core model affecting cost/security/outcomes are notified in advance.
  4. Cost control. The Platform uses model routing / small-model-first / caching / token optimisation; Party A provides a monthly AI usage and cost report.

12. Confidentiality

Each Party owes a confidentiality obligation over the other's non-public information (including Platform technical information, commercial terms and learner information) obtained in the Project, used only to perform this PSA, surviving until the information is public or lawfully disclosed; this clause survives termination for [3-5] years, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

13. Warranties & disclaimers

  1. Each Party warrants it has authority to enter into and perform this PSA.
  2. Party A warrants the Platform services will be provided with reasonable skill and care, materially conforming to the Schedule 1 MVP requirements.
  3. Disclaimer. To the maximum extent permitted by law, save as expressly stated, Party A gives no express or implied warranties (including merchantability or fitness for purpose); it does not guarantee any learner's exam results or official HSK certification; and it does not warrant uninterrupted or error-free operation.

14. Limitation of liability

  1. To the maximum extent permitted by law, no Party is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential loss, or loss of profit/goodwill/data.
  2. Each Party's aggregate liability under this PSA is capped at [the amounts received/payable by the liable Party under this PSA in the preceding 12 months / an agreed cap].
  3. Exceptions to the cap (not limited by 14.1–14.2): breach of confidentiality, breach of data/child protection, IP infringement, wilful misconduct, and liability that cannot be limited by law.

15. Indemnities

  1. Party A indemnifies the other Parties, within this PSA's liability framework, against claims that the Platform itself infringes third-party IP (conditioned on permitted use, prompt notice and Party A controlling the defence); Party A may modify/replace/obtain a licence for, or terminate, the affected part.
  2. Each Party indemnifies the others against third-party claims arising from its breach of data/child protection or confidentiality, or its gross negligence.

16. Term, suspension & termination

  1. This PSA takes effect on signature and runs until the end of Phase 1 / renewal.
  2. On material breach uncured after [30] days' written notice, the non-breaching Party may terminate; on a Party's insolvency/cessation, the others may terminate immediately.
  3. On termination: the licence ceases immediately; data/content/configuration are handed over per Clause 10; sums are settled for services rendered; Clauses 5, 9, 10, 12, 14, 15 and any provision that by its nature survives, continue.

17. Change control

Any material change to functionality/scope requires a written Change Request stating the change, reason, impact on system/time/fees/data-security/AI, and each Party's approver; no obligation arises from an unapproved change, and new features adjust fees and timeline accordingly.

18. General

  1. Assignment/subcontracting. No assignment without the others' written consent; Party A may subcontract but remains responsible for its subcontractors.
  2. Force majeure. Non-performance caused by force majeure (including large-scale local power/network outages, natural disaster, war, government action) is not a breach; the affected Party notifies and mitigates.
  3. Notices. Notices must be in writing to the Parties' [addresses/emails].
  4. Entire agreement / variation / waiver / severability. This PSA with the Framework and Schedules is the entire agreement; variations must be in writing; a waiver is not a continuing waiver; invalidity of one provision does not affect the rest.
  5. No partnership / agency. The Parties are independent contracting parties; this PSA creates no partnership, joint venture or agency.
  6. Third-party rights. Save as expressly provided, the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 2001 does not apply, and a non-party has no right to enforce this PSA.
  7. Counterparts. This PSA may be executed in counterparts, including electronically, together forming one instrument.

19. Governing law & dispute resolution

This PSA is governed by the laws of Singapore and construed together with Nigerian local law; disputes are referred to the Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) under its rules in force, seat Singapore, [one / three] arbitrator(s), language [Chinese / English]. This clause survives termination.
