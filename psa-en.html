Project-Specific Agreement (PSA)
Single-country / single-school pilot

Schedule 1 to the Tripartite Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement · Governing law: Singapore · v1.0 (draft, subject to legal review)

Draft — for review only. This is a project-specific template under the Tripartite Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement (the "Framework"), used to record the commercial terms, responsibilities, fees and settlement for one specific country/school. The Framework sets the principles; this PSA sets the specific numbers and arrangements for the project. In case of conflict, commercial terms are governed by this PSA, while compliance / IP / dispute-resolution and other principle-level terms are governed by the Framework. This document does not constitute legal advice and must be reviewed, localised and finalised by a Singapore-qualified lawyer. Square brackets [ ] are to be completed.

6. Brand & intellectual property

  1. The intellectual property in the AI Chinese learning platform's technical works (code, algorithms, the JE Engine, the agent architecture, TIDAR-KAG, interface design, etc.) belongs to Party A (JE AI) and is made available to the Project under a non-transferable licence per the Framework.
  2. The "SHANHAI TONGWEN" brand belongs to Party C (the Association) and is used within the Project under the Brand Licence Agreement, subject to quality control and revocable.
  3. Ownership and licensing of pedagogical works created in the Project follow Clause 5 of the Framework and are detailed in [schedule].

7. Service levels & acceptance

  1. Availability/support: platform availability target [%]; ticket response [time]; weak-network degradation (audio-first/offline) is an agreed capability.
  2. Acceptance: pilot reviewed against the Delivery Plan's success criteria; a measured unit-cost figure is produced.

8. Term, changes & termination

  1. This PSA runs from signature until the end of the pilot / renewal.
  2. Changes must be in writing; on a material breach uncured within [days], the non-defaulting Party may terminate.
  3. On termination: data handled per the DPA, sums settled for services rendered, brand and technology licences cease immediately.

9. Relationship to the Framework

This PSA is Schedule 1 to the Framework, and together they form the entire agreement. Commercial terms (fees/splits/scale) are governed by this PSA; compliance, data, child protection, IP and brand, and dispute resolution are governed by the Framework. Matters not covered here are governed by the Framework.
