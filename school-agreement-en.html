Parties & recitals

Project side (Association side): Global Association of Cultural and Educational Exchange ("GACEE" / the "Association"), owner of the SHANHAI TONGWEN master brand [and / or local delivery entity; JE AI as platform/technology provider], [reg. no. / address]

School: [school — full name / country / address / authorised representative]

Whereas the Parties intend to run and potentially continue a SHANHAI TONGWEN AI Chinese learning platform cooperation at [school] in [country]; and to make the commercial transition from pilot to rollout seamless, they enter into this school-facing Agreement derived from the Framework Agreement.

1. Definitions & the two-phase + conversion structure

  1. Two phases: this Agreement runs in a "Phase 1 · Pilot" (low-commitment, easy exit) and a "Phase 2 · Rollout" (formal use and fees).
  2. Conversion: after the pilot, the School signs the Order Form (Schedule A) to activate the Rollout — no new contract and no re-negotiation.
  3. Pre-locked commercial terms: the Rollout price, seats and term are pre-agreed and price-locked in Schedule A at the signing of this Agreement; conversion does not change the price (unless both sides agree in writing).
  4. Phased binding: during the Pilot, only Clauses 6 (data & child protection), 7 (brand & IP) and 8 (confidentiality) are binding; once the Rollout is activated it becomes fully binding (see Clause 10).

2. Phase 1 · Pilot

  1. Scope & scale: [20–40] learners; [6]-week term; weekly [1 live class + 1 recording + daily practice]; dates [ ].
  2. Deliverables: recordings + live class + offline practice + tutor / review agents + learning reports + completion certificates (issued by the Association, verifiable).
  3. Pilot fee: [free / nominal]; third-party direct-cost bearing per Schedule A (and the internal PSA).
  4. Low commitment & exit: either side may exit on [ days ] written notice, with no liability beyond actual costs incurred; the pilot is no procurement commitment.
  5. Success criteria: assessed item-by-item against Schedule B, as the objective basis for deciding whether to convert.

3. Conversion mechanism (Order Form)

This clause is the commercial heart. It compresses the "second close" from a fresh negotiation to a one-page Order Form.
  1. Results report: [ days ] before the pilot ends, the Project side provides a pilot results report against Schedule B.
  2. Conversion (opt-in): to convert, the School's authorised representative signs the Order Form (Schedule A), which activates the Rollout; once signed it becomes an integral part of this Agreement.
  3. Price lock: Rollout commercial terms follow the pre-agreed Schedule A — no re-negotiation, no price rise on conversion (price-lock valid until [ ]).
  4. If not converted: if the School does not sign the Order Form within [ period ], the pilot ends amicably with no further obligations (save Clauses 6 / 7 / 8); no auto-renewal or auto-conversion to paid applies.
  5. [Optional clause · off by default] Silent conversion: only where both sides expressly agree in writing and local law permits may they provide that "the Rollout begins automatically unless objected to before expiry." Because this Agreement concerns services to minors and (government) school procurement, this method is not used by default, to preserve trust and governance compliance.

4. Phase 2 · Rollout (commercial terms)

  1. Pricing model: [per seat / month · per class · per-school package]; unit price per Schedule A.
  2. Term & renewal: Rollout term [semester / school year]; renewal is by actively signing a renewal Order Form — again no default auto-renewal.
  3. Service levels: platform availability target [%]; ticket response [time]; low-bandwidth degradation (audio-first / offline) as an agreed capability.
  4. Support & training: teacher training [sessions]; support channel [ ]; local teaching-assistant collaboration.
  5. Scale changes: seat increases / decreases adjust at the Schedule A unit price, reconciled monthly, non-retroactive.

5. Collection & settlement

  1. Collecting entity: Rollout service fees are collected by [collecting entity: JE AI / local commercial entity]; no individual may collect on the side.
  2. No commingling of Association funds: any Association grants, donations or entitlements go to an account in the Association's name, with zero commingling with commercial funds; the Association distributes no surplus from commercial services (see the Compliance · Legal · Finance advisory).
  3. Settlement: reconciled every [month / quarter] with vouchers; disputed amounts held over, the rest paid on time.
  4. Internal split — not the School's concern: the internal delivery split among the Project side (Association / JE AI / resource party) is governed by the Framework and the internal PSA; the School is not a party to those arrangements.

6. Data & child protection (binding across both phases)

Minors are involved; this is a hard, binding clause spanning both the pilot and the rollout.
  1. Obtain verifiable parent / guardian consent before processing learner data; process under the Data Processing Addendum (DPA) and local data law [VN PDPD / ID UU PDP / MY PDPA / SG PDPA].
  2. Observe the Child Safeguarding Policy; personnel in contact with learners are background-checked and trained.
  3. Data minimisation; cross-border transfers must have a lawful basis.

7. Brand & IP (binding across both phases)

  1. The AI Chinese learning platform's technical works (code, algorithms, JE Engine, agent architecture, TIDAR-KAG, interface design, etc.) belong to JE AI, licensed for this cooperation on a non-transferable basis.
  2. The "SHANHAI TONGWEN" brand belongs to the Association; the School's use must comply with the Brand Licence Agreement and the Brand Book; the English form must be the full "SHANHAI TONGWEN", never a bare "SHANHAI".
  3. The School shall not use the brand or platform outside the scope of this cooperation.

8. Confidentiality (binding across both phases)

Each side owes a confidentiality obligation over the other's non-public information and learner information obtained in the cooperation, surviving until the information is public or lawfully disclosed.

9. Term, exit & termination

  1. This Agreement takes effect on signature; the Pilot and Rollout terms are set in Clause 2 and Schedule A respectively.
  2. Pilot exit per Clause 2.4; in the Rollout, on a material breach uncured after [ days ], the non-breaching side may terminate.
  3. On termination: data handled per the DPA, sums settled for services rendered, brand and technology licences cease immediately.

10. Effect (phased binding) & disputes

  1. Phased binding: during the Pilot only Clauses 6, 7 and 8 are binding; once the Order Form (Schedule A) is signed, the Rollout terms become fully binding.
  2. Governing law & disputes: this Agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore together with local law; disputes go to SIAC or a competent local forum agreed by the Parties, in [Chinese / English]. This clause survives termination.
