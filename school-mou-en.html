School Pilot Cooperation MOU
学校试点合作备忘录

Derived from the Framework · Governing law: Singapore + local law · v1.0 (draft, subject to legal review)

Draft — for review only. This MOU records the intent and arrangements between the Association (and the project side) and a pilot school to run a SHANHAI TONGWEN AI Chinese learning platform pilot. Except for the binding clauses expressly stated (Clause 7 Confidentiality, Clause 4 Data & child protection), this MOU is generally non-binding; binding commercial terms are set out in the separately signed Project-Specific Agreement (PSA). It is not legal advice and must be localised against local law. Square brackets [ ] require completion.
Positioning: this MOU is the light fallback, for schools that cannot pre-agree formal terms at the pilot stage (e.g. must re-tender); where a school can pre-agree, use the School Cooperation Agreement (Pilot-to-Rollout) instead — pilot→rollout in one signature, price-locked schedule, conversion by a single Order Form — for a smoother commercial transition.

Parties

Project side: Global Association of Cultural and Educational Exchange ("GACEE" / the "Association") [and / or local entity, JE AI]

School: [school — full name / country / address / authorised representative]

1. Purpose & nature

  1. Purpose: a [6]-week Chinese-teaching pilot at the school, to validate teaching effectiveness and operational feasibility under real campus and network conditions.
  2. Nature: this MOU is a statement of intent and arrangements; specific commercial terms (fees, settlement, splits) are governed by the PSA.

2. Pilot arrangements

  1. [20–40] learners; [6]-week term; weekly [1 live class + 1 recording + daily practice]; dates [ ].
  2. Deliverables: recordings + live class + offline practice + learning reports + completion certificates (issued by the Association, verifiable).

3. Responsibilities

  1. Project side: provide the platform & AI, pedagogy, remote teachers and teacher sign-off, certificates; technical support and training.
  2. School: organise learners, fix the timetable, obtain parent/guardian consent, provide venue and shared devices, appoint a local teaching assistant, and support network/devices.

4. Data & child protection

Minors are involved; this clause is a hard, binding requirement.
  1. Obtain verifiable parent/guardian consent before processing learner data; process under the Data Processing Addendum (DPA) and local data law.
  2. Both sides observe the Child Safeguarding Policy; personnel in contact with learners are background-checked and trained.

5. Brand & IP

  1. Platform technical works belong to JE AI (non-transferable licence); the "SHANHAI TONGWEN" brand belongs to the Association, and the school's use within the pilot must comply with the Brand Licence Agreement and the Brand Book.
  2. The school shall not use the brand or platform outside the pilot scope.

6. Fees

  1. The pilot is [free of charge / as agreed]; third-party direct-cost bearing is set out in the PSA.

7. Confidentiality

Each side owes a confidentiality obligation over the other's non-public information and learner information obtained in the cooperation (this clause is binding and survives until the information is public or lawfully disclosed).

8. Term & termination

  1. This MOU runs from signature until the end of the pilot / renewal.
  2. Either side may terminate on [ days ] written notice; termination does not affect accrued confidentiality, data and child-protection obligations.

9. Effect & disputes

Except for Clauses 4, 5 and 7, this MOU is a statement of intent and non-binding. For disputes on the binding clauses, the Parties negotiate in good faith; failing that, the laws of Singapore apply together with local law, and disputes go to SIAC or a competent local forum agreed by the Parties.
