Schedule 1 to the Project-Specific Agreement (PSA) · Applies to the Africa and Southeast Asia pilots · v0.1 (draft skeleton)

Draft skeleton — internal & negotiation use. This Annex sets out the functional and technical requirements the Platform is to meet for the Project, as Schedule 1 to the PSA. Important: this Annex only states "what capabilities the Platform provides"; it contains no IP, source-code or data-ownership clauses — those are governed uniformly by the PSA: the Platform is Party A's Background IP provided under a non-transferable licence (PSA cl. 5); business continuity is delivered by data/content portability, with no core source-code escrow (PSA cl. 10); data ownership is per the DPA. Features are marked MVP Phase 1 / later / PoC subject to trial results. Square brackets [ ] require completion.
A. Architecture & modules B. Roles · profile · content C. AI capabilities D. Teaching delivery E. Non-functional F. Analytics & learning loop G. AI governance & safety H. Data & cyber security I. Service levels & DR J. Integration · scale · cost K. Acceptance & phasing X. Not addressed here

BRoles · learner profile · content

  • Roles & permissions: learner / teacher / school admin / country (regional) project admin / global project admin / super admin; supports "global → country → institution → school → class → learner" hierarchy. MVP
  • Digital learner profile: continuously updated learning profile (particulars, ability dimensions, attendance/assignment/assessment, AI records, engagement, strengths/weaknesses, goals & ILAP, HSK progress), updated dynamically from learning data. MVP
  • Content management: course/unit/lesson/objectives/vocab/characters/grammar/listening-speaking-reading-writing/quizzes/assignments/mock exams; mappable to HSK levels and ability indicators; architecture supports HSK updates without a rebuild. MVP

DTeaching delivery (live & recordings)

  • Live classes: integrate with approved third-party meeting platforms (Zoom / Google Meet / [Voox etc.]); create/schedule/link/notify/attendance/online-time/class-link; semi-automatic attendance where third-party APIs are limited. PoC (cross-border weak-network RTC & local path)
  • Recordings: classified/retrievable by country→school→class→course→date→teacher; resume/low-res download/offline viewing; later auto-captions/transcript/chapters/content search.

FAnalytics & learning loop

  • Four-tier analytics: learner / teacher / school / country (global); export Excel / CSV / PDF. MVP
  • Learning loop: teach → practise → assess → diagnose → personalise → intervene → re-assess → update ILAP → report; moving from "how much viewed" to "what was actually learned and what to learn next".
  • Early warning: detect consecutive absence/incomplete assignments/declining activity/behind schedule; alert teacher or admin. MVP
  • HSK progress & readiness tracking; the Platform's "readiness/prediction" must not be represented as official HSK certification or official results.

GAI governance & learner safety

  • Human oversight: AI analyses/suggests/assists; authorised teachers/admins retain final teaching judgment; may modify/override/correct/report/flag AI outputs; important AI determinations are not permanently locked without human review.
  • Child safety: where minors are served, AI safety guardrails apply; no unsuitable/manipulative/inappropriate-emotional-dependency content; the AI companion is positioned as a learning tool, not a substitute for human relationships.

HData protection & cyber security

Per PSA Schedule 5 (DPA) and local law (Africa: Nigeria NDPA; SEA: Vietnam PDPD / Indonesia UU PDP / Malaysia PDPA / Singapore PDPA). At least: authentication, least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, admin MFA, activity logs & audit, retention & deletion, consent; secure development/vulnerability management/patching/monitoring/intrusion protection/backup/DR/incident response; material breaches notified within the DPA time limits. MVP

IService levels & disaster recovery

Target monthly availability [99.5%] (excluding planned maintenance); severe/normal fault response, repair times, RTO/RPO per PSA Schedule 3. Platform faults and "local internet/power outages" are treated separately, not as the same SLA responsibility (see PSA cl. 7). Automatic backup / database / content / configuration backup + periodic recovery testing.

JIntegration · scale · cost · search

  • API integration: standardised, secure APIs supporting meeting platforms/WhatsApp/SMS/payment/identity/mobile app/other education & analytics platforms.
  • Scalability: architecture supports progressive scaling to [1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000] learners; servers/DB/AI/bandwidth/storage and cost at each scale "subject to actual testing and a then-current quote", with no rebuild on growth. to test
  • AI cost control: model routing, small-model-first, token optimisation, caching, call-volume metering; monthly AI usage & cost report. MVP
  • Search: global search subject to permission control. later

KAcceptance & phasing

  • Phasing: Phase 1 delivers only the MVP scope above to a usable standard; PoC items are decided by trial results; the rest are later.
  • Acceptance (aligned with PSA cl. 6 + Schedule 4): the Parties jointly set acceptance criteria and UAT cases at initiation, fitted to local low-bandwidth/(power-outage)/low-spec realities. Testing includes at least: functional, API, integration, cyber-security, performance, low-bandwidth, offline, network-recovery, (power-recovery), low-spec Android, AI, AI error-rate/anomalous-output, UAT.
  • Technical documentation: system architecture, DB schema, API docs, admin/teacher/learner manuals, AI architecture & agent descriptions, security/backup/DR/deployment docs.
