SHANHAI TONGWEN · GACEE · Tripartite Agreement

Optimized Clauses 15–17 — Term, Termination & Non-Circumvention
Association-Protective Redraft · Governing law: Singapore

Replaces Clause 7 (Resource Registration & Anti-Circumvention) and Clause 12 (Term, Termination & Exit) of the Tripartite Strategic Cooperation Framework Agreement.

PURPOSE OF THIS REDRAFT. Built on the counterparty's Replacement Clauses, but re-cut to maximise protection of the Association (GACEE). Three structural upgrades: (1) the restrictions now bind every Non-Association Party — i.e. the Technology Partner and the Resource Partner — not the Technology Partner alone (the counterparty draft left the Resource Partner, arguably the higher flight-risk, entirely unbound); (2) remedies escalated from injunction-only to injunction + liquidated damages + account of profits + audit + Association step-in; (3) a Platform-IP / customer-relationship separation so that ownership of the platform cannot be used to claim the students, schools or customers introduced through the cooperation. Green = new protection; amber = materially changed. Bracketed [ ] fields require completion.
Defined term used throughout. "Non-Association Party" means each of the Technology Partner (Party A / JE AI) and the Resource Partner (Party B), and any of their respective affiliates. "Association" means Party C (GACEE). Terms not defined here have the meaning given in the main Agreement.

15. Term and Termination

  1. 15.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the date specified above and continues unless and until terminated in accordance with this Clause 15. Expiry or termination is subject to Clauses 16 and 17.
  2. 15.2 Termination for Convenience. Subject to Clause 15.3, any Party may terminate this Agreement on not less than [60] days' prior written notice to the other Parties. A termination for convenience by a Non-Association Party does not release it from, or reduce, its obligations under Clauses 16 and 17, which continue in accordance with their terms.
  3. 15.3 Protection of Ongoing Projects. Notwithstanding Clause 15.2:
    1. termination of this Agreement shall not automatically terminate or prejudice any PSA then in force;
    2. each PSA then in force continues in accordance with its terms, including for the remainder of its agreed term, unless and until terminated under the express termination provisions of that PSA;
    3. where a PSA has no express termination provision, it may be terminated only by mutual written agreement of the relevant Parties or for material breach under Clause 15.4; and
    4. a Party terminating this Agreement remains responsible for performing its obligations under all PSAs that survive such termination.
    For the avoidance of doubt, a Party shall not rely on termination of this Agreement to avoid, terminate or reduce its obligations under an existing PSA.
  4. 15.4 Termination for Cause. A Party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if another Party:
    1. commits a material breach which, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within [30] days after written notice requiring remedy;
    2. commits a material breach of Clauses 6–10 or Clause 17 which is incapable of remedy or, if capable, is not remedied within [30] days;
    3. becomes insolvent or enters liquidation, judicial management, administration or any analogous proceeding; or
    4. engages in fraud, wilful misconduct or other conduct which materially damages, or is reasonably likely to materially damage, the reputation or legitimate interests of the terminating Party or the Association.
  5. 15.5 Termination by a Non-Association Party. Each Non-Association Party acknowledges that the commercial relationships, student base, school and institutional relationships, market opportunities and Projects developed through the cooperation are subject to the protections in Clause 17. Accordingly, no Non-Association Party shall terminate this Agreement, any PSA or its participation in any Project for the purpose of, or as a means of, acquiring, retaining, transferring or continuing any Protected Business or Protected Customer independently of the Association.
  6. 15.6 No Termination to Avoid Restrictions. Any purported termination effected in breach of Clause 15.5 is without prejudice to the Association's rights under Clause 17, and the restrictions and obligations in Clause 17 continue to apply notwithstanding such termination.
  7. 15.7 Termination of a PSA. Where a PSA is terminated, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement an orderly transition that protects students, schools and other customers and minimises disruption to the Programme. No Party shall, during or after such transition, use the transition process to solicit, divert or transfer Protected Customers or Protected Business away from the Association in breach of Clause 17. The Association may exercise its step-in rights under Clause 16.7 in respect of any terminated PSA.

16. Effect of Termination

  1. 16.1 General Consequences. On expiry or termination of this Agreement or any applicable PSA: (a) the relevant Technology and Brand licences cease to the extent provided in the applicable PSA and this Agreement; (b) each Party ceases using the others' Confidential Information, Brand and proprietary materials except as necessary to perform surviving obligations, effect an orderly transition or comply with law; (c) each Party returns or securely deletes Confidential Information and Personal Data under Clause 11 and the DPA, subject to lawful retention; (d) accrued rights and liabilities survive; and (e) clauses intended by nature to survive continue in force.
  2. 16.2 Existing PSAs. Unless expressly terminated under its terms, each PSA in force at the date of termination continues until its scheduled expiry or earlier lawful termination. During that period: (a) the Technology Partner continues to provide the Platform, technology and operational support allocated to it; (b) each Non-Association Party continues to comply with all obligations on student protection, data protection, reporting, accounts, fee collection and other matters for the relevant Project; (c) the Association continues to provide its required services; and (d) no Party takes any action that would materially prejudice the continuation or orderly completion of the Project.
  3. 16.3 Transition. On expiry or termination, the Parties cooperate in good faith for an orderly transition of affected Projects, including where applicable: (a) notifying affected schools, students, parents, guardians and stakeholders; (b) ensuring continuity of student services for the transition period; (c) transferring or making available information reasonably necessary for continued delivery or orderly completion of the Programme, subject to data-protection law; (d) reconciling all student fees, Project funds, outstanding invoices and other amounts; and (e) completing any certificates, records or student-facing obligations accrued before termination.
  4. 16.4 No Appropriation of Students or Customers. No Non-Association Party shall, during any transition period or following expiry or termination: (a) represent that it has acquired or succeeded to the Programme, the Brand or the Association's business; (b) encourage or procure any Protected Customer to terminate, reduce or cease its relationship with the Association for the purpose of transferring that relationship to a Non-Association Party; (c) transfer students or schools from the Programme to a competing programme operated by or for a Non-Association Party; or (d) otherwise circumvent the Association in respect of any Protected Business or Protected Customer.
  5. 16.5 Fees, Accounts and Audit. Termination does not affect any obligation to account for or pay fees, revenues or amounts accrued before the effective date. Each Non-Association Party shall provide a final reconciliation of all student fees and Project amounts collected or received by it or on its behalf under the applicable PSA. The Association may, on reasonable notice and at its cost (or at the breaching Party's cost where a breach is established), appoint an independent auditor to verify such reconciliation and compliance with Clauses 16 and 17; the audited Party shall provide reasonable access to relevant records.
  6. 16.6 Survival. Clauses 11, 13, 16 and 17, and any provision intended by nature to survive, survive expiry or termination.
  7. 16.7 Association Step-In and Continuity. On termination of this Agreement or any PSA, or on a material breach of Clause 17 by a Non-Association Party, the Association may, at its option and to protect students, schools and the Programme: (a) designate itself or a substitute provider to assume continued delivery of the affected Projects; and (b) require each Non-Association Party to cooperate in good faith to hand over and migrate, in an orderly and lawful manner, the student records, enrolment data, school and parent contact points, certificates and other information reasonably necessary for continuity (subject to the DPA and applicable data-protection law). Nothing in this Clause transfers ownership of the Technology Partner's Platform IP; it secures continuity of the Programme and the Association's Protected Business and Protected Customers.

17. Non-Circumvention, Non-Solicitation and Restricted Activities

  1. 17.1 Protected Business and Protected Customers. For this Clause 17:
    1. "Protected Business" means the Programme, each Project and each business opportunity, school, educational institution, government or governmental agency, distributor, partner, sponsor, student or other customer or counterparty introduced, developed, sourced, referred to or otherwise brought within the scope of the cooperation by or through the Association or the Resource Partner;
    2. "Protected Customer" means any school, educational institution, government or governmental agency, distributor, partner, sponsor, student, parent or guardian, or other customer or counterparty which: (i) has been introduced to a Non-Association Party by or through the Association or the Resource Partner; (ii) has participated in, enrolled in, contracted for or otherwise been involved in a Project or the Programme; (iii) is identified or contemplated in any PSA or approved Project plan; or (iv) has been the subject of material discussions, negotiations or business-development activities in connection with the Programme or a Project during the Term;
    3. "Restricted Activity" means providing, operating, marketing, promoting or developing a Chinese-language education programme or substantially similar education service which competes with the Programme or any Project and which makes use of, or is targeted at, Protected Business or Protected Customers; and
    4. "Restricted Territory" means each country or territory in which a Project has been undertaken, is being undertaken, or is the subject of an executed PSA during the Term.
  2. 17.2 Non-Circumvention. During the Term and thereafter in accordance with Clause 17.7, no Non-Association Party shall, directly or indirectly, whether for its own account or through any affiliate, nominee, intermediary, employee, consultant, agent, distributor or other person: (a) circumvent, bypass or seek to circumvent or bypass the Association in relation to any Protected Business or Protected Customer; (b) use any introduction, relationship, customer or student information, school relationship, market opportunity or other commercial connection obtained through the cooperation to establish, develop or pursue a business relationship independently of the Association; (c) enter into, negotiate, solicit, accept or perform any arrangement with a Protected Customer in respect of a Restricted Activity other than under an applicable PSA or with the Association's prior written consent; or (d) structure any transaction or relationship for the purpose or effect of avoiding the restrictions in this Clause 17.
  3. 17.3 No Circumvention Through Termination. No Non-Association Party shall use the expiry or termination of this Agreement or any PSA to obtain, retain or exploit any Protected Business or Protected Customer independently of the Association. In particular, no Non-Association Party shall: (a) terminate this Agreement after a substantial student or school base has been established and thereafter continue providing substantially the same programme or services to such students or schools independently of the Association; (b) encourage or procure Protected Customers to continue with a Non-Association Party after termination outside an arrangement approved by the Association; (c) use access to student, parent, school or institutional relationships developed through the Programme to establish a competing programme; or (d) transfer, migrate or redirect students, schools or other Protected Customers to a competing service operated by a Non-Association Party or any affiliate.
  4. 17.4 Non-Compete During the Term. During the Term, no Non-Association Party shall, without the Association's prior written consent, directly or indirectly undertake any Restricted Activity in a Restricted Territory other than under this Agreement or an applicable PSA.
  5. 17.5 Non-Solicitation Following Termination. For [12] months after expiry or termination, no Non-Association Party shall, directly or indirectly: (a) actively solicit any Protected Customer for a Restricted Activity; (b) actively solicit students enrolled in the Programme to transfer to a competing programme operated by or for a Non-Association Party; (c) induce or procure any Protected Customer to terminate, reduce or materially alter its relationship with the Association for the purpose of engaging a Non-Association Party independently; or (d) represent to any Protected Customer that a Non-Association Party has acquired or succeeded to the Programme, the Brand or the Association's rights.
  6. 17.6 Protection of Student and Customer Data. Each Non-Association Party shall use student, parent, school and institutional information obtained through the Programme solely for the purposes for which it was collected and in accordance with data-protection law, the DPA and the relevant PSA. Following expiry or termination, no Non-Association Party shall use such information for marketing, solicitation, customer migration or the development of a competing programme, except as expressly permitted by applicable law and the relevant data-protection arrangements.
  7. 17.7 Duration. The restrictions in Clauses 17.2, 17.3 and 17.4 apply during the Term and for [12] months following expiry or termination. The obligations concerning (a) Confidential Information; (b) misuse of student or Personal Data; (c) use of the Brand and Programme materials; and (d) diversion or appropriation of Protected Business or Protected Customers through misuse of Confidential Information or other proprietary information, continue for so long as the relevant information remains confidential or otherwise protected by law.
  8. 17.8 Platform IP and Customer-Relationship Separation. The Parties acknowledge that the Technology Partner owns the Platform and its IP under Clause 5. However, ownership of the Platform or its IP does not confer on any Non-Association Party any right, title or interest in the Protected Business or Protected Customers, or in the student, school, parent or institutional relationships introduced or developed through the cooperation, all of which remain protected under this Clause 17. No Non-Association Party shall assert its Platform or IP rights as a basis to retain, service or solicit any Protected Customer otherwise than in accordance with this Agreement.
  9. 17.9 Reasonableness and Severability. Each restriction in this Clause 17 is separate and severable. If any restriction is held unenforceable by reason of duration, geographical scope, activity or otherwise, it shall apply to the maximum extent permitted by law, and the Parties consent to any reduction a court or tribunal considers necessary to render it enforceable. Each Non-Association Party acknowledges that the restrictions are reasonable and necessary to protect the Association's legitimate interests, its non-profit mission, and the students, schools and Programme it stewards.
  10. 17.10 Remedies. The Parties acknowledge that a breach or threatened breach of this Clause 17 may cause the Association irreparable harm for which damages may be inadequate. Accordingly the Association is entitled, without prejudice to any other right or remedy:
    1. to injunctive relief, specific performance and other equitable relief for any actual or threatened breach, without proof of special damage and without any requirement to post security;
    2. to liquidated damages of [ ] (or, if greater, an amount equal to [100]% of the revenue derived by the breaching Party from the circumvented or diverted Protected Business or Protected Customer), which the Parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of loss and not a penalty;
    3. to an account of, and payment over of, all profits and benefits obtained by the breaching Party from the breach; and
    4. to recover its reasonable costs (including legal costs on an indemnity basis) of enforcing this Clause 17 where a breach is established.
